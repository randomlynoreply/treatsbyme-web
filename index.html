Common Questions

How far in advance should I order? +

Please enquire as early as you can, especially for popular dates such as school holidays and long weekends. I'll confirm whether your date is available.

Do you cater to dietary requirements? +

Please mention any dietary requirements in your enquiry and I'll let you know what's possible.

Do you deliver? +

Please mention in your enquiry whether you would like pickup or delivery, and I'll confirm what's possible for your cake and location.

How do booking & payment work? +

Booking and payment details are confirmed with you personally once your cake and date are agreed.
