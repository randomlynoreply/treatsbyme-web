About the Baker

Where Imagination
Meets Celebration

Every creation has a story, and this is where ours began.

The cake you see here was the very first one she created for her daughter. What started as a mother’s love for making her little girl’s celebration extra special soon blossomed into a passion for bespoke sweets and savouries.

With a creative imagination and a keen eye for detail, our baker believes every celebration deserves something truly special. From beautifully crafted cakes to delightful sweet and savoury creations, each piece is thoughtfully made to bring a little magic to life’s memorable moments.

Every creation begins with your imagination.

Whether you have a specific theme, a unique concept or just the beginnings of an idea, she’ll work closely with you to bring your vision to life. Through a personalised creative process, your ideas are transformed into bespoke creations that reflect your style, your occasion and your story.

Because the most magical celebrations are made in the details.
