Made for Your Wedding Day

Every wedding is different, and so is every cake. From simple, elegant ivory tiers with sugar roses to multi-tier designs with ruffle detail and fresh flowers, each wedding cake is designed and handmade to suit your celebration.

Some couples have a clear design in mind. Others would love a little inspiration. Share your ideas, and we’ll talk through your style, your day and the details that matter to you.

Wedding cake pops, cupcakes and dessert tables can be created to sit alongside your cake.
