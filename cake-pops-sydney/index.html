Small Treats, Big Details

Cake pops are a lovely way to carry your theme across the table. From delicate sugar flowers and ribbon bows for a wedding to favourite characters for a kids’ party, each batch is decorated to suit your celebration.

They pair beautifully with a custom cake, cupcakes and cookies, so your colours and design stay consistent from one treat to the next.

Share your event, your theme and any inspiration, and we’ll discuss the details with you personally.
